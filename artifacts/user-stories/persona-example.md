# Sample User Persona
## E-Commerce Shopper - Busy Professional Priya

---

### Demographics
- **Age**: 34
- **Occupation**: Marketing Manager
- **Location**: Urban, hybrid work
- **Devices**: Primarily mobile (iPhone), occasional laptop
- **Shopping frequency**: 2-4 online purchases per month

---

### Goals
- Find products quickly without creating accounts
- Complete checkout in under 3 minutes
- Clearly understand total cost and delivery date before paying
- Avoid having to contact support

---

### Frustrations (Current Experience)
- Forced to create an account mid-checkout
- Shipping costs appear too late
- Hard to resume a previous browsing session on mobile
- Product recommendations feel irrelevant

---

### Behaviors
- Starts shopping on mobile during commute or lunch
- Compares 2-3 options before deciding
- Abandons cart if checkout feels long or unclear
- Prefers guest checkout + email receipt

---

### Quote
> I just want to buy the thing and move on. Dont make me fill out a form for an account Ill never use.

---

### Implications for Requirements
- Guest checkout is essential
- Mobile-first progressive flow
- Early visibility of shipping & total
- Persistent cart / recently viewed across sessions
