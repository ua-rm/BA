# Stakeholder Communication Plan
## Sample - E-Commerce Modernization Project

---

## 1. Communication Objectives
- Keep stakeholders aligned on progress, risks, and decisions
- Reduce ad-hoc status requests
- Ensure the right people receive the right level of detail at the right time

---

## 2. Stakeholder Communication Matrix

| Stakeholder Group | Information Needs | Channel | Frequency | Owner | Format |
|-------------------|------------------|---------|-----------|-------|--------|
| Executive Sponsor (CDO) | Progress against objectives, major risks, decisions required | Email + 15-minute call | Bi-weekly | BA + Product Owner | 1-page status report + RAG status |
| Product / E-Commerce Lead | Backlog health, scope changes, upcoming decisions | Working session | Weekly | BA | Agenda + decisions log |
| Engineering Lead | Refined stories, dependencies, technical risks | Refinement sessions + Slack | 2-3 times per week | BA | Story details + open questions |
| UX Lead | Requirements clarity, acceptance criteria, feedback | Design critique sessions / Miro | As needed | BA | Annotated flows + acceptance criteria |
| Marketing & Support | Launch impact, training needs, known limitations | Email + Sprint Review | Sprint cadence | BA | Summary + FAQ |
| Wider Organization | High-level progress and key wins | Newsletter / Slack | Monthly | Product Owner | Short update |

---

## 3. Status Report Cadence (Example)

**Bi-weekly Executive Status (template sections)**
1. Overall health (RAG)
2. Progress against key objectives / metrics
3. Accomplishments this period
4. Focus for next period
5. Risks & issues (with owners)
6. Decisions required

---

## 4. Escalation Path
1. BA attempts resolution with relevant leads
2. Unresolved after 48 hours -> Product Owner
3. Cross-team or budget impact -> Executive Sponsor

---

## 5. Tools
- Status & decisions: Confluence / SharePoint
- Day-to-day: Slack / Teams
- Formal approvals: Email or signed decision log
