## 2. RACI Matrix

| Activity / Decision                      | CDO | Head of E-Com | Eng Lead | UX Lead | Marketing | Support | BA |
|------------------------------------------|-----|---------------|----------|---------|-----------|---------|----|
| Approve business objectives & success metrics | A   | C             | C        | I       | I         | I       | R  |
| Define MVP scope                         | C   | A             | C        | C       | C         | I       | R  |
| Prioritize backlog (MoSCoW)              | I   | A             | C        | C       | C         | I       | R  |
| Approve UX designs                       | I   | C             | C        | A       | C         | I       | C  |
| Technical architecture decisions         | I   | I             | A        | C       | I         | I       | C  |
| Go / No-Go for launch                    | A   | C             | C        | C       | I         | I       | R  |
| Post-launch success review               | A   | R             | C        | C       | C         | C       | R  |

**Legend**  
- **R** = Responsible (does the work)  
- **A** = Accountable (final decision / owns outcome)  
- **C** = Consulted (provides input)  
- **I** = Informed (kept up to date)

## 3. Engagement Approach

| Stakeholder Group | Preferred Communication | Frequency | Primary Contact |
|-------------------|-------------------------|-----------|-----------------|
| Executive (CDO) | Decision brief + metrics dashboard | Bi-weekly | BA + Head of E-Com |
| Product / E-Com | Working sessions + backlog review | Weekly | BA |
| Engineering | Technical refinement, spikes | 2-3x / week during refinement | BA + Tech Lead |
| UX | Design critique & usability findings | As needed / sprint | BA + UX Lead |
| Marketing & Support | Impact updates + training needs | Sprint review + ad-hoc | BA |
