# Project 01: E-Commerce Platform Modernization

**Domain**: Retail / E-Commerce  
**Role Demonstrated**:  Business Analyst  
**Focus**: Requirements Elicitation, Documentation & Agile Backlog Creation

---

## Business Context

A mid-sized online retailer is experiencing declining conversion rates and high cart abandonment. Leadership has approved a modernization initiative for the checkout and product discovery experience. The Business Analyst is responsible for clarifying the problem, eliciting requirements from multiple stakeholders, and producing a prioritized backlog ready for development.

### Key Business Objectives
- Reduce cart abandonment rate from 68% to under 50% within 6 months
- Improve mobile conversion rate by 25%
- Decrease average checkout time by 30%
- Maintain or improve customer satisfaction (CSAT >= 4.2/5)

## Key Techniques Used

- Stakeholder analysis & RACI
- Facilitated requirements workshop
- User story mapping & MoSCoW prioritization
- Acceptance criteria (Given/When/Then style)
- Requirements prioritization against business objectives
