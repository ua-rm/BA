# Project 03: Customer Churn Insights

**Domain**: Subscription / SaaS or Retail Loyalty  
**Role Demonstrated**:  Business Analyst  
**Focus**: Data Exploration, Insight Generation, Recommendation Development

---

## Business Context

Customer churn has been rising for three consecutive quarters. Leadership wants to understand *who* is leaving, *why* patterns exist, and *what actions* can be taken. The Business Analyst partnered with the data team to explore available data, define meaningful segments, and produce actionable insights.

### Key Questions
- Which customer segments have the highest churn risk?
- What early signals precede churn?
- Which interventions are most likely to improve retention?
